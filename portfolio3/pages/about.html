A little about my background

I am an IT undergraduate who has spent the past few years building experience across three different but connected worlds — event operations, creative production, and administrative support. That combination is what makes me useful to event planning clients specifically: I understand how events work from the inside.

I have worked in an actual event catering environment, managed social media and produced content for a brand, written scripts and screenplays for live school productions and runway shows, and served as an officer in an academic organization handling documentation and coordination. Each of those roles sharpened a different skill that I now bring together.

I am organized by nature, comfortable working independently across time zones, and currently building this portfolio to connect with remote clients in the events, creative, and professional services space.

How I position myself

I sit between administrative precision, creative production, and technical capability — which makes me particularly useful to event planners, content brands, and small remote teams who need one reliable person who can cover a lot of ground.
